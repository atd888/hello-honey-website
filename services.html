A little help choosing

Good to know.

What is the difference between gel and BIAB?

Gel polish adds a cured colour finish. BIAB stands for builder in a bottle: a builder gel used to give natural nails additional structure. If you're unsure which to request, describe what you'd like in your booking notes and Hello Honey can discuss the options before confirming.

What if I already have product on my nails?

Tell us whether you have gel polish, builder gel, acrylic or another product, even if it was applied elsewhere. Select gel removal if needed and describe the existing product in your notes so Hello Honey can confirm whether removal is suitable and agree the time and price before your visit.

How much will my visit cost?

Your treatment price, any travel costs and timing are agreed before your appointment is confirmed. Include your postcode, removal needs and any nail art ideas in your request. Submitting a request does not reserve a slot, and no payment is taken at this stage.
