Requests, confirmation and payment

Sending the form on our booking page is an appointment request, not a confirmed booking. We check availability and postcode coverage, then contact you with the details. You do not pay when you send the request.

If we can accept your request, we will email you a secure Stripe payment link for a 40% deposit. Your appointment is confirmed only after the deposit has been paid and Hello Honey has confirmed the appointment details with you. The remaining balance, when it is due and how to pay it will be confirmed with you before the appointment.

Changing or cancelling an appointment

Please email us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel. Changes depend on availability. Our deposit refund rule and instructions for cancelling are on the cancellations and deposits page. Your legal cancellation rights are separate and still apply.

For online service bookings, you may have a legal right to cancel within 14 days after the booking contract is made. If you want the appointment to take place within those 14 days, we will ask you separately to request this in writing. If you then cancel after the service has started, you may have to pay a proportionate amount for the service already provided, where the law allows. If the service is fully completed after your request and acknowledgement, the legal cancellation right may end when it is complete.
