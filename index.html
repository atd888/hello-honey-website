Good to know

Your questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

Gel manicure £32, BIAB manicure £42, BIAB infills £38, acrylic full set with gel polish £40 and gel pedicure £42. Nail art starts from £5. There is no travel charge, and your final price is agreed before your appointment is confirmed.

Which areas do you cover?

We mainly visit homes across North London, including Hornsey, Crouch End, Muswell Hill, Highgate, Wood Green and Finsbury Park, plus nearby areas. Coverage is checked by postcode when you send your request.

When are appointments available?

Monday to Sunday, 10am to 4pm, subject to availability. Tell us your preferred day and time and we will confirm a slot with you.

How long does an appointment take?

As a guide, allow 60–75 minutes for a gel manicure, 75–90 minutes for a BIAB manicure and 60–75 minutes for a pedicure. Removal and nail art can add time.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

No. There is no deposit and no online payment. You pay after your treatment, by cash or bank transfer.

I already have gel or BIAB on my nails. Is that OK?

Yes — just tell us what is on your nails when you book. Gel removal is £15 on its own or £8 with another treatment, and BIAB removal is £20 on its own or £12 with another treatment.
