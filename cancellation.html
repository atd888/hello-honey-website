A little clarity before your appointment

Changes & cancellations.

No deposit is required. Please tell us promptly if you need to change or cancel your appointment.

Before your appointment is confirmed

Sending an appointment request does not confirm a booking. You can withdraw your request at any time before Hello Honey Mobile Nails accepts it, without paying a cancellation charge.

Changing or cancelling your appointment

Please email artemidaballiu35@gmail.com or call 07424 883538 as soon as you can. Include your name and appointment date. Changes depend on availability. No deposit is collected for new appointments; payment is due after your treatment by cash or bank transfer.

If you paid a deposit under an earlier booking, contact us about that existing booking. This change does not remove any refund or cancellation rights agreed for an earlier payment.

Your legal cancellation rights

When you book online, you may have a legal right to cancel within 14 days after the booking contract is made. This right is not removed by our booking process. To use this right, email us before the 14-day period ends with a clear message that you want to cancel.

If you ask us to start the service during that 14-day period and then cancel, you may have to pay a proportionate amount for work already carried out, where the law allows. If the service is fully completed during that period, the cancellation right ends only where you asked us to start early and acknowledged that the right would end when the service was complete.

Where a refund is due under your legal cancellation rights, we will make it within 14 days using the original payment method.

How to contact us

Email artemidaballiu35@gmail.com. You can also write to Hello Honey Mobile Nails, the trading name of Artemida Balliu, at 94 Lausanne Road, N8 0HP, United Kingdom.

You can use this wording in your email: “I am cancelling my Hello Honey Mobile Nails appointment. My name is [your name], and my appointment date is [date].”
